0 m · The problem

Today, a fault underground takes days to reach the manager and weeks to reach the regulator.

Compliance lives in paper registers and spreadsheets spread across mines, contractors and field offices. By the time anyone reads the evidence, it is old.

No trail from the field

Observations, violations and corrective actions sit in registers. Nobody sees them until someone reads the register.

No early warning

Recurring failures go unnoticed until something goes wrong. There is no layer that watches the pattern.

Reports made by hand

Statutory reports to DGMS and SPCB take days to compile, and the same numbers are copied between spreadsheets.

        −420 m · The AI layer

        AI that warns early and shows its reasoning.

        Built on Google Gemini and the Agent Development Kit. The AI advises. People decide.

        How a risk score is made Illustrative

        0
        High
        risk score out of 100

        Move the inputs to see the score and the reasons change. The real score reads live mine data.

        • Risk scoringScores every mine from violations, closure rates, gas readings and production pressure.
        • Anomaly and pattern detectionFlags recurring violations and unusual readings before they become incidents.
        • Statutory report draftingFills reports from real data. A person reviews and signs.
        • Grievance understandingTranscribes, translates, categorizes and prioritizes worker voice notes in five languages.
        • Human in the loop, everywhereLow-confidence OCR goes to review. Corrective actions need evidence. Reports need a digital signature.

        Built to run, and to scale

        This is a working prototype, not a mockup. Adding a mine means adding configuration, not rebuilding software.

        Field appReact Native and Expo. Offline store, geo-tagging, QR scan, voice.
        BackendFastAPI with Supabase: Postgres, PostGIS and Realtime updates.
        Dashboards and mapsReact with MapLibre and DeckGL, for five roles.
        AI agentsGoogle Gemini and ADK, reading the same records as the dashboards.
