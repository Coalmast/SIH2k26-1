AI Agents
Five agents. One coherent system.
  • 🎯
    Risk Scoring
    Scores every mine from violations, closure rates, gas readings and production pressure.
  • 🔍
    Anomaly & Pattern Detection
    Flags recurring violations and unusual readings before they become incidents.
  • 📝
    Statutory Report Drafting
    Fills reports from real data. A person reviews and signs before submission.
  • 🗣
    Grievance Understanding
    Transcribes, translates, categorizes and prioritizes worker voice notes in five languages.
  • 👤
    Human in the Loop, Everywhere
    Low-confidence OCR to review. Reports need digital signature. AI advises — people decide.
Architecture

Built to run. Built to scale.

A working prototype, not a mockup. Adding a mine means adding configuration, not rebuilding software.

React Native & Expo

Backend
FastAPI + Supabase
